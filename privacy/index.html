Thrift Flip

Privacy policy

Effective September 25, 2026

Thrift Flip ("the game") is made by Paper Kite Game Studios ("we"). This policy explains what the game collects, why, and the choices you have. Contact: trainmessage@gmail.com.

The short version

  • The game has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, contacts, photos, or location.
  • Ads play only when you tap an offer to watch one. Google's advertising services collect some data to show and measure those ads.
  • Anonymous gameplay stats are optional. Where GDPR applies they are off unless you say yes; everywhere, you can switch them off in Settings.

Ads (Google AdMob)

The game offers optional rewarded ads through Google AdMob. When ads are allowed, Google's Mobile Ads SDK collects your device's advertising ID, your IP address (from which an approximate location can be inferred), your interactions with ads, and diagnostic information, to serve and measure ads and to prevent fraud. Google's own use of this data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Before any ad loads, the game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to ask for your consent where the law requires it, including in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland, and US states with privacy laws. You can change that choice at any time from Settings › Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Anonymous gameplay stats (PostHog)

If you allow it, the game sends gameplay events to PostHog, an analytics service, on servers in the European Union. The events describe play, such as which item you restored, which lot you bought, what you sold it for, whether you watched a rewarded ad, and script errors the game hit, along with the time of each event, the game version, and a random ID made on your phone when you installed the game. They never include your advertising ID, your name, or anything you typed. PostHog is set not to store IP addresses.

We use these stats to learn which parts of the game people enjoy and to fix bugs. Where GDPR applies, the game asks before sending any, and sends nothing unless you say yes. Everywhere, you can switch them off in Settings; switching off also deletes any stats still waiting on your phone. We keep stats for 12 months and then delete them.

Sharing your before/after picture

When you tap Share, the game makes the picture on your phone and opens Android's share sheet. The app you choose receives the picture under that app's own policy. The game uploads nothing itself.

Data stored on your phone

Your saved game, your stats choice, stats waiting to send, the random install ID, and your last before/after picture are stored in the game's private storage on your phone. Uninstalling the game deletes them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or object to the processing of your data. Because the stats are anonymous, we can find yours only with the ID shown in Settings under the stats switch; send it to our contact address with your request. For data Google collects for ads, see Google's policy linked above. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed at children and is intended for players 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the game, contact us and we will delete any stats linked to the ID they give us.

Changes

We will post any change to this policy on this page and update the effective date.
